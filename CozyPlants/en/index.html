Inside your garden

A plant game to help you relax

Looking for a calm game for your phone? Cozy Plants is an idle gardening game made for mindfulness: no timers chasing you and nothing to lose. You grow at your own pace and the garden keeps growing when you close the app.

Every plant in the herbarium is hand-drawn in a warm comic style and comes with its real scientific name and fun facts, so you learn a little botany while you collect.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Cozy Plants free?

Yes. Cozy Plants is a free download on the App Store and includes optional in-app purchases.

What kind of game is it?

A relaxing idle (incremental) collecting game: grow plants, complete a 24-species herbarium and decorate your garden and shed, with no pressure.

Do I need an account?

No. Cozy Plants needs no sign-up or login: your progress is stored on your device.

Which languages does it support?

English, Spanish and French.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
