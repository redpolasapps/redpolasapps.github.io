Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Cozy Plants mobile application (the "Application") developed and operated by Victor M. Garcia Ortega (the "Service Provider").

Gameplay Data & Local Storage

The Application does not require account creation, social logins, or personal identity verification. All your game progress, garden layouts, plant evolution stages, inventory items, and customization settings are stored locally on your device (or synchronized securely via your private Apple iCloud account if enabled). The Service Provider does not transmit, store, or process your gameplay records on external private servers.

Information Collection, In-App Purchases, and Advertisements

The Application integrates trusted third-party SDKs to manage digital transactions, optional rewarded video rewards, and ad placements. These service providers may automatically collect technical, non-personally identifiable diagnostic and device metrics, including:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Mobile advertising identifiers (such as IDFA where authorized via App Tracking Transparency)
  • Device hardware model, operating system version, and system language
  • Basic in-game diagnostic and crash logs to maintain game performance

Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its respective privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob & UMP (Advertisements & Consent Management)
  • Apple StoreKit & In-App Purchases

Tracking and European Privacy Consent (GDPR / UMP)

For players residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage user consent for ad personalization and identifier usage. You can review, modify, or revoke your consent preferences at any time through the in-game settings menu or via your iOS device settings (under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Data Deletion and Reset

Because your gameplay progress is held on your physical device, uninstalling the Application removes all local game files, plants, and elixirs. If you wish to request the deletion of any email correspondence or support inquiries sent to the Service Provider, please contact us at redpolasapps@gmail.com.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 16 years of age. If you believe that a minor has provided personal details through support channels, please contact us at redpolasapps@gmail.com so we can remove the communication promptly.

Security

The Service Provider enforces standard operational and technical practices to ensure the security and integrity of all data processed within the Application ecosystem.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect new features or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
