Why track your dog's weight?

Weighing your dog regularly is one of the simplest ways to look after their health: weight changes, up or down, are often one of the first signs that something has changed in their diet, activity or overall condition.

DogWeight keeps every weigh-in in one place, with a chart, so you can show it to your vet and make decisions with real data. It's especially useful for growing puppies, dogs on a diet and senior dogs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is DogWeight free?

Yes. DogWeight is a free download on the App Store and includes optional in-app purchases.

Can I track more than one dog?

Yes. Create a profile for each dog with a photo, breed and birthdate, and keep each one's history separately.

Can I share the history with my vet?

Yes. Export the weigh-ins as a PDF report or a spreadsheet (CSV).

Where is my data stored?

On your device. No account is needed. If you like, you can keep a backup in your own Google Drive.

Does it replace my vet?

No. DogWeight is a personal tracking tool: its data and charts are not a diagnosis and don't replace a visit to a licensed veterinarian.

DogWeight is a personal tracking tool for pet owners. Its data and charts are not veterinary advice, diagnosis or treatment.
