Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the The Line Tycoon mobile application (the "Application") developed and operated by Victor M. Garcia Ortega (the "Service Provider").

Gameplay Data & Local Storage

The Application does not require account creation, social logins, or personal identity verification. All your game progress, stars, album stickers, custom parks, and settings are stored locally on your device. The Service Provider does not operate any server for the Application and does not collect, transmit, store, or process your gameplay records.

Advertisements

The free version of the Application shows advertisements, including optional rewarded videos, provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure those advertisements, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect technical data from your device, including:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be derived
  • Device and advertising-related identifiers
  • Device hardware model, operating system version, and system language
  • Information about the advertisements shown and your interaction with them
  • Diagnostic data from the advertising software

When you first open the Application, iOS asks whether you allow it to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, the advertising software can use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you more relevant advertisements and to measure them. If you don't, the IDFA is not available to the Application or to its advertising partners, and the game works exactly the same. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Players who bought the Premium pack are not asked.

Players who buy the one-time Premium pack no longer see advertisements.

In-App Purchases

The Application offers a single one-time purchase (the Premium pack). Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The Service Provider never receives or stores your name, payment card, or billing details; the Application only learns whether the purchase is active on your Apple account.

Photos

If you choose to save or share a picture of your park, the Application asks for permission to add that picture to your photo library. The Application cannot read your existing photos, and the pictures you save or share are not sent to the Service Provider.

Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its respective privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob & UMP (Advertisements & Consent Management)
  • Apple StoreKit & In-App Purchases

European Privacy Consent (GDPR / UMP)

For players residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage your consent before advertisements are requested. Where this consent applies, you can review, modify, or withdraw it at any time from the in-game settings menu (Privacy & ads).

Data Deletion and Reset

Because your gameplay progress is held on your physical device, uninstalling the Application removes all local game files. If you wish to request the deletion of any email correspondence or support inquiries sent to the Service Provider, please contact us at redpolasapps@gmail.com.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 16 years of age. If you believe that a minor has provided personal details through support channels, please contact us at redpolasapps@gmail.com so we can remove the communication promptly.

Security

The Service Provider enforces standard operational and technical practices to ensure the security and integrity of all data processed within the Application ecosystem.
